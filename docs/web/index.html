Duebook

Family udhaar, group expenses and a small service business, in one simple app. English and Urdu.

خاندان کا ادھار، گروپ کا خرچ اور کاروبار کا حساب، ایک ہی ایپ میں۔

What it does

Personal ledger

Who owes you and who you owe, with reminders and a shareable statement. A big button simple mode for elders.

Shared groups

Split expenses with family or friends and see who has settled.

Jobs for your team

Schedule installations and repairs, alert the team, and watch each job move from confirmed to finished.

Bill on the spot

Pick the items used, make the invoice, share the PDF on WhatsApp. Unpaid bills land in the customer's ledger automatically.
