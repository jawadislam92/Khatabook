Duebook privacy policy

Effective 9 November 2026

Duebook is a ledger and small business app made by Sparking Asia, Rawalpindi, Pakistan. This page explains what information the app stores, why, and what you can do about it. We keep it short and plain on purpose.

What we store

  • Your account. Email address, display name, preferred language, and an optional phone number you choose to show. If you sign in with Google, Google shares your name, email address and profile picture with us and nothing else.
  • What you enter. Names, phone numbers and addresses of the people and customers you add, ledger entries and notes, receipts or photos you attach, groups, jobs, items, invoices and payments.
  • Your devices. A push notification token for each phone you sign in on, so alerts can reach it.
  • Activity records. Who changed what inside a shared ledger or business space, shown to the members of that space.

We do not collect your location, your contacts list, or advertising identifiers. There are no ads and no analytics trackers in the app.

Why we store it

To run the app: keep your records safe, sync them between your devices and the people you have chosen to share a ledger or business space with, send the reminders and job alerts you asked for, and produce invoices and statements. We do not sell your information and we do not use it for marketing.

Who can see it

  • You, and the members you invite to a shared ledger or business space. A manager of a business space sees the jobs, customers and invoices of that business.
  • Service providers that host the app on our behalf: Supabase (database and sign in, servers in Mumbai, India), Google Firebase Cloud Messaging (delivers push alerts; only the device token and the alert text pass through it), and Amazon Web Services (holds our encrypted nightly backups).
  • Authorities, only if the law requires it.

How it is protected

Data travels encrypted and is stored encrypted. Each record is tied to the spaces you belong to and the database refuses requests for anything else. The app offers a PIN or fingerprint lock, and devices that stop responding are removed from the alert list.

How long we keep it

As long as your account is active. When you delete your account from the app's Settings, or ask us by email, your account and the records you own are removed within 30 days. Entries you made inside a space owned by someone else stay with that space, without your name attached. Backups roll off within 30 days after that.

Your choices

  • See and change your details in the app's Settings.
  • Turn alerts off in your phone's notification settings or inside the app.
  • Export your ledger as a PDF statement at any time.
  • Ask us to send or delete your data by writing to the address below.

Children

Duebook is not meant for anyone under 13, and we do not knowingly keep information about children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version appears here with a new effective date. For a change that matters, we also tell you inside the app.
